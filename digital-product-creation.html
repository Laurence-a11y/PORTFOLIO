LAURENCE PANGILINAN
Digital Art

Digital Product Creation: Minimal Wallpapers with Modern Aesthetics.

I focus on creating digital experiences that transform everyday devices into curated spaces. By balancing negative space with subtle gradients and geometric forms, I craft wallpapers that feel intentional, modern, and aesthetic. It is about bringing calm to the digital workspace.

Design Principles

  • Minimalism Eliminating distractions to focus on core visual elements and clean compositions.
  • Color Harmony Using muted tones and intentional palettes to evoke a sense of calm and sophistication.
  • Modern Geometry Exploring the intersection of sharp lines and soft shadows to create depth on a flat screen.
